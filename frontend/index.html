Reference-forest contrasts against the cohort median; the displayed probability comes from the saved backend model.

    This is not a medical test

    The model learned from 768 records of Pima women aged 21 and over, collected in the 1980s. It reflects that group, not the general population, and it is wrong roughly one time in five. Use it to explore how the features interact, not to decide anything about your health. For that, see a doctor.

    About the model

    Live prediction

    The live result comes from the existing backend/model.pkl: a fitted scikit-learn pipeline with a zero-value imputer and an XGBoost classifier. The backend applies that saved preprocessing before inference; it does not retrain or replace the model.

    The pipeline was saved without feature labels. Its eight-value order is the order defined by the existing frontend exporter. Skinfold is converted from millimetres to inches, and unmeasured optional values are sent as missing for the fitted imputer.
